Commands

What you can ask the bot to do. Type !bot in the server or channel chat first: it messages you privately, and you send everything else in that private chat. Commands aren't case-sensitive, so it doesn't matter how you type them. Tip: click any command to copy it.

Getting started

If the bot restarts, or you reconnect to the server, your private chat with it stops working — send !bot again to open a new one. If a message from the bot turns up in a chat where your replies don't seem to go anywhere, close that chat tab and send !bot again. In a public chat only !bot and !coinflip work, once every 10 seconds per person. In the bot's messages, commands you can type show in bold, so it's clear where they end.

!bot
Wakes up the bot and opens a private chat with you. Type it in the server chat or your channel's chat — it's the only way to reach the bot, since it's hidden in the channel list.
!help
Sends you a link to this page, and points you to !helplist.
!helplist
Lists everyone's commands in chat, one line each.
!whoami
Shows whether your TeamSpeak account is linked to a supporter, and gives you your TeamSpeak ID to send to the Owner if you need linking.

Perks

!perks
Shows everyone with a perk right now: how many banner and modal weeks they have left, whether a running week is currently active and when it ends, and whether their mute shows "Ready" or a date it comes back.
!perks <name>
Shows just one person's perks, for example yours.
!control status
Shows who currently holds the banner and the modal, and when each of their weeks ends.
!banner start
Starts your week of banner control straight away. Needs the perk: see Perks. Only one person can hold it at a time — if someone else already has it, you're told when their week ends so you know when to try again. You'll get a message when you're down to your last 24 hours, and again when your week ends.
!banner set <picture address> [link]
Puts up your banner picture while you hold it, with an optional link people can click through to. Needs the perk: see Perks. Upload your picture first to one of a few sites that don't leak the IP address of everyone who views it — Postimages, ImgBB, Catbox or Gyazo — then use its direct picture link (ending in .png, .jpg or .gif). Imgur doesn't work here, since it blocks UK visitors.
Example: !banner set https://i.postimg.cc/abc123/my-banner.png
!modal start
Starts your week of modal control — the pop-up message everyone sees when they connect. Needs the perk: see Perks. Only one person can hold it at a time — if someone else already has it, you're told when their week ends so you know when to try again. You'll get a message when you're down to your last 24 hours, and again when your week ends. Heads up: an important service modal from the owner can interrupt it, and nobody can start a new week while it's up. Your week pauses while it's up and picks up where it left off once it's gone, so you lose no time.
!modal set <text>
Sets the pop-up message while you hold it: up to 200 characters, and each line break counts as 2. Links are fine but each counts 11 more than it looks; pictures aren't allowed. To start a new line, press Shift+Enter in the chat box. If it's too long, the bot says so and saves nothing. Heads up: an important service modal from the owner can interrupt yours, pausing your week, which picks up where it left off once the service modal is gone.
Example: !modal set Manchester United is better than Liverpool!
!mute <name>
Silences someone online for 1 hour; it lifts on its own after that. Anyone can be muted, staff included, and nobody can lift it early. Needs the perk: see Perks.

Twitch

!twitch link <your channel>
Asks to link your Twitch channel, so your name shows [LIVE] while you're streaming. Send just your channel name, or its twitch.tv address. The bot checks with Twitch that the channel exists, then the Owner has to approve it; you get a message when they do, if you're online. Linking a different channel later replaces the old one and needs approving again. If you use TeamSpeak on more than one device, link each one separately — every device needs its own link.
Example: !twitch link https://twitch.tv/yourname
!twitch
Shows your current link and whether it's been approved yet.
!twitch unlink
Removes your link, and takes off [LIVE] straight away if you're streaming.
!twitch show
Lists you on the site's Live now page while you're streaming, with a link to your channel and when you went live. Nobody's listed unless they ask, since the page is public to anyone online. Can take about 10 minutes to catch up after you go live or stop. If you link a different channel afterwards, send !twitch show again to reappear on the page.
!twitch hide
Takes you off the Live now page again. Your [LIVE] tag in TeamSpeak works the same either way.

Locking a channel

For a private lobby or your community channel, when you want to play without being disturbed. A locked channel shows [LOCKED] in its name and gets a hidden password so nobody else can walk in; anyone who somehow gets in is moved straight back out. A lock also ends on its own once the channel has been empty for 2 minutes.

!lock
Locks the channel you're in and protects everyone inside at that moment: nobody can poke them, message them first, move them, kick them or ban them (they can still reply if someone messages them first). Friend, Mandem, Lémön Crew and higher ranks can use it in any private lobby or community channel, but Associates can't. A community channel's Leader and Channel Admins can also lock that channel. It's refused in Entrance Hall, AFK, the public lobbies and spacers, if you're on your own in there, if the channel already has its own password or is already locked, if someone in it is in jail, or if you already have a lock running somewhere else.
!unlock / !unlock <channel>
Ends the lock: the name and password go back to normal. Use it in the channel itself, or name the channel to end its lock from anywhere on the server. You can unlock a lock you started; in a community channel, its Leaders can end any lock there. Part of the name is enough — capitals, spaces and symbols like # don't matter — and if it matches more than one locked channel, the bot lists them so you can be more specific.
Example: !unlock private lobby 2
!rejoin
Moves you back into your locked channel if you stepped out on purpose (a dropped connection is put back automatically once you reconnect). You have 10 minutes before you lose your place. If whoever locked the channel isn't back within that time, the lock ends for everyone; if it's someone else who isn't back in time, they just lose their place and the lock carries on.

Coinflip

The result always posts in your channel's chat, whether you typed it there or in your private chat with the bot. In the few channels the bot doesn't sit in, such as AFK, you'll get the result as a private message instead. Once every 10 seconds per person, and you can't play from a locked channel.

!coinflip
Flips a coin and posts the result in your channel for everyone to see.
!coinflip heads / !coinflip tails
Calls it before the flip, so the bot can say whether you won.

Trivia

You get a game together in TeamSpeak, then play it on a web page: when the game starts, the bot sends each of you your own link to it. On your page you see each question with a countdown and four answers, and everyone's score, and you click an answer (or press 1 to 4). A wrong answer turns red on your page only; the first right answer ends the question, and everyone sees the right answer in green, who got it and the scores. When a new question comes up the page plays a short chime (you can switch it off). You can't start or join a game from a locked channel, and a game where only one person answered doesn't count on the leaderboard. When a game ends, press Play again on your page to open a new one on the same topic in the same channel: everyone there is invited, and the last game's players can join straight from their page.

!trivia
Opens a 10-question game of mixed topics in the channel you're in, or joins one already running there. You're its host, and everyone else in the channel gets an invite to join. Once your game has started, it sends you your link again, from any channel.
!trivia <topic>
Opens a game on one topic instead. If a game is already running in your channel, you join it, and the bot tells you if it's on a different topic. Capitals and spaces don't matter.
Example: !trivia football
!trivia topics
Lists the topics: general, geography, history, science, games, movies, tv, anime, sport, football, basketball and cs (Counter-Strike: the pro scene, the games, maps and fun facts; !trivia cs2 and !trivia csgo work too).
!trivia join
Joins the game in your channel; before it starts, everyone already in is told. You can also join after it's started: the bot sends you your link, and you start from the next question.
!trivia start
Starts the game once everyone's in. Only the host (the person who opened it) can do this; if they leave first, the next person in takes over as host. A game nobody starts closes 1 minute after it's opened. Everyone then gets a link to their own page, mandem.arazez.com/trivia, and question 1 comes up 15 seconds later so everyone has time to open it. Keep it to yourself: anyone with your link can answer for you. You get 20 seconds per question.
!trivia leave
Leaves the game, the same as Leave on your page. Before it starts, you're simply out. After that, your points so far still count, and the others play on.
!trivia top
Shows the all-time top 10 in chat. It's also on the Leaderboards page.

Uno

For 2 to 10 people. You get a game together in TeamSpeak, then play it on a web page: when the game starts, the bot sends each of you your own link to it. On your page you see the top card, everyone's card counts, whose turn it is with a countdown, the last few moves and your own cards, and you click a card to play it. You can't start or join a game from a locked channel. Leaving TeamSpeak doesn't take you out of the game, but miss 3 turns in a row and you're out. When it becomes your turn the page plays a short chime (you can switch it off), and you can press D to draw and P to pass. When a game ends, press Play again on your page to open a new one in the same channel: everyone there is invited, and the last game's players can join straight from their page.

!uno
Opens an Uno game for 2 to 10 people in the channel you're in, or joins one already open there. Once your game has started, it sends you your link again.
!uno join
Joins the open game before it starts. Nobody can join once it's under way.
!uno start
Starts the game once everyone's in — only the person who opened it can do this (if they leave first, the next person in the game takes over as host). A game nobody starts closes 1 minute after it's opened. Everyone then gets a link to their own page, mandem.arazez.com/uno. Keep it to yourself: anyone with your link can play your cards. You have 60 seconds per turn: if time runs out you draw a card.
!uno quit
Leaves the game, the same as pressing Quit twice on your page (!uno leave works too). It counts as a loss, but your cards still count toward whoever wins.
!uno top
Shows the all-time top 10 in chat, with games won and lost and win rate. It's also on the Leaderboards page.

Chess

Play chess against someone else on the server, on Lichess, a free chess website with no adverts. The bot sets the game up and sends each of you a link, and you play in your browser on a proper board with clocks. Neither of you needs a Lichess account. You can't play from a locked channel.

!chess <name> / !chess <name> <minutes>
Challenges someone who's online. Each of you gets 10 minutes on the clock, or put the minutes after the name (1 to 60) for a quicker or longer game. Part of the name is enough. They have 1 minute to answer, or the challenge lapses and you're told. You can only have one challenge waiting at a time.
!chess accept
Accepts a challenge sent to you. You each get a link for your colour, and white moves first. If you haven't both opened your links within an hour, the game is off. The first time two people play each other, the bot picks white at random; after that you take turns. When the game ends, the bot tells whichever of you is online and updates your ratings, usually within a minute.
!chess decline
Turns down a challenge sent to you.
!chess cancel
Takes back your challenge before it's answered.
!chess top
Shows the top 10 by rating in chat, with games won, drawn and lost. Everyone starts at 800, like a beginner on chess.com, and a win takes points from the loser: more for beating someone rated higher, fewer for someone rated lower. You're on it after your first game. It's also on the Leaderboards page.

For community channel leaders

Only for a community channel's Community Leader or Channel Admin. Run these from inside your channel. Leaders can also end a lock in their channel that someone else started, with !unlock <channel>. If nobody online matches the name you give to !role, the bot searches everyone who's ever used the server by the name they last used, and tells you when they were last on so you can check it's the right person. If more than one matches, give their TeamSpeak unique ID instead — get yours with !whoami — to pick the exact account.

!role add <name> <member|admin>
Makes someone a Member or Channel Admin of your channel, and lets them in. Only the server owner can make someone a Community Leader.
Example: !role add Bob member
!role remove <name>
Takes someone's role in your channel away, so they're a guest again.
!pull <name>
Brings someone into your channel from wherever they are on the server (except AFK). It won't pull anyone into a locked channel — unlock it first. A Community Leader in their own channel can't be pulled out.
