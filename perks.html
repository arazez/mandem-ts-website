Perks

Supporters who chip in toward the server's yearly costs get perks on the server. Here's what each one does, how to earn it, and who has what right now.

Want perks? Every donation helps keep the server online. Go to Supporters →

What each perk does

🖼️ Banner

The image shown on the right in the channel details page, visible to everyone on the server, with an optional link people can click. You choose it for a week.

Send !banner start to the bot, then !banner set <picture address> [link]. The picture has to be on Postimages, ImgBB, Catbox or Gyazo, so nobody can see who viewed it.

💬 Modal

The pop-up message everyone sees when they connect. You write it for a week: up to 500 characters, links are fine, pictures aren't.

Send !modal start, then !modal set <your message>.

🔇 Mute

Silence anyone on the server for 1 hour. It lifts by itself.

Send !mute <name>. Used between October and March, it comes back on 1 April. Used between April and September, it's gone until the next October. Leftover mutes clear every 1 October.
